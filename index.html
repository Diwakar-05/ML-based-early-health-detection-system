    Built for screening, not guessing

    • Early detection

      Models trained on public clinical datasets pick up risk patterns before a diagnosis.

    • Explains every result

      SHAP values show which of your readings mattered most, so the score is never a black box.

    • Result in seconds

      Fill a four-step form and get your report right away, with advice for next steps.

    • Your data stays yours

      Readings are used only to calculate your risk and are saved only if you log in.
